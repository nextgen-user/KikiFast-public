# Kiki Health Companion — handoff

## Product purpose

Kiki is a companion-robot platform intended to support everyday routines,
communication, engagement, and general wellbeing. It is not a medical device
and must not be presented as one.

## Major capabilities

- Voice-driven interaction and device control.
- Camera and sensor-assisted context.
- Configurable routines and senior-care experiences.
- Optional family communication and external integrations.
- Local dashboard visibility for device status and events.

## UI guidance

The companion interface should prioritize clarity, large touch targets, simple
language, and predictable navigation. Important states should be understandable
at a glance and should not depend on color alone.

Suggested areas include a home summary, routine planning, event timeline,
health and environment summaries, contacts, and device setup.

## Safety and privacy

- Do not make diagnostic or emergency-response claims.
- Clearly distinguish sensor readings from verified medical information.
- Require confirmation for consequential actions.
- Minimize collection of personal data and keep it out of source control.
- Design offline and degraded states explicitly.

## Engineering handoff

Use the public interfaces and configuration examples in this repository.
Deployment-specific services, credentials, and private runtime components are
provided separately by the project maintainers when required.
