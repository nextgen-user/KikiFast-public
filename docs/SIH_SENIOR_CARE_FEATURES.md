# Senior-care feature overview

## Included product concepts

- Configurable reminders and daily routines.
- Guided conversation and lightweight activities.
- Optional health-device and environment context.
- Family contact and notification workflows.
- Timeline and dashboard summaries.
- Accessible setup and interaction modes.

## Experience principles

- Ask before taking consequential actions.
- Use calm, direct, and respectful language.
- Make uncertain or unavailable data obvious.
- Keep the user in control of routines and contacts.
- Avoid medical conclusions from consumer sensors.
- Continue safely when optional services are offline.

## Deployment notes

Features depend on the configured hardware and services. Credentials, personal
records, device identifiers, and runtime-generated data must remain outside Git.

This document intentionally describes product behavior at a high level. Private
runtime implementations and detailed operational logic are distributed
separately by the maintainers.
