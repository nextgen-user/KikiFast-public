# Senior-care roadmap

## Goal

Develop supportive daily experiences that are respectful, configurable, and
safe for older adults and their families.

## Priorities

1. Reliable reminders and routine planning.
2. Accessible voice and display interactions.
3. Family-approved communication workflows.
4. Clear health and environment summaries.
5. Graceful offline and hardware-degraded operation.
6. Transparent privacy controls and data retention.

## Delivery phases

- Foundation: configuration, routine data, accessibility, and safety language.
- Companion experiences: reminders, guided activities, and check-ins.
- Context: optional sensor and environment signals with clear limitations.
- Family experience: summaries, contacts, and consent-aware notifications.
- Validation: usability, reliability, privacy, and hardware testing.

## Non-goals

The system does not diagnose conditions, replace caregivers, guarantee emergency
response, or provide clinical monitoring.

Detailed implementation plans and internal evaluation evidence are maintained
outside the shared repository.
